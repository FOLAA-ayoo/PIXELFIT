How it works

Private. Your images are processed in your browser and are never uploaded.

Limit the file size. Pixelfit lowers quality until the file fits under your limit. PNG is lossless, so choose JPEG or WebP for size limits.

If the limit can’t be met, reduce the width and height, then try again.

Transparency. JPEG fills transparent areas with white. Use PNG or WebP to keep them.
